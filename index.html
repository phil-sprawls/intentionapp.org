How it works

From thought to action in seconds

01

Tap the widget

The lock screen widget opens Intention directly — before you get to your home screen.

02

State your intention

Type what you need to do, or tap a common action. No menus, no navigation.

03

Go do it

Intention routes you to the right app or action and gets out of the way.

04

Mark it done

Close the loop when you finish. Your phone session had a purpose — and you kept it.
